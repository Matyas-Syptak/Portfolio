How to Play

  • Move: A/D or Arrow Keys. On phone, use the left/right buttons.
  • Jump: W, Space, Up Arrow, or the mobile jump button.
  • Collect SlimeDrops of size 1-5. Starting mass is 10; each drop adds half its shown value: 1 = +0.5 mass, 5 = +2.5 mass. Missing hearts are restored by collecting drops, not by waiting.
  • Stomp enemy slimes from above. Only a clearly larger attacker, at least 1.5x the victim's size, gets a 1-stomp kill. Similar = 2 stomps. Smaller = 3 stomps.
  • Watch the eyes. Cross eyes mean a hit landed. A defeated slime bursts into SlimeDrops.
  • Survive. Big enemy slimes are rare early, then become more common as your mass pushes you into new Spore-like scale zones.
