This folder contains the published website files. index.html is the home page.

Preview on your computer

1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open a terminal in the extracted Design-Portfolio folder.
3. Run python3 -m http.server 8000 (or py -m http.server 8000 on Windows).
4. Open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.

Host elsewhere

Upload the contents of Design-Portfolio as your static site's root. Keep the
styles, scripts, images, and games folders beside index.html. Serve the files
over HTTP or HTTPS so the site can load its animation modules.

The site loads its original typefaces from Google Fonts when online.
Links to papers open their original sources. NightShift Drive uses WebGL 2.
