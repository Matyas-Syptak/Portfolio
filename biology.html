Kuang Lab

Professor Shihuan Kuang · Duke University

I help investigate how PTEN affects skeletal muscle stem cells and muscle regeneration. My work includes PCR genotyping, myoblast culture, and immunofluorescence staining and imaging of muscle cryosections. I analyze fluorescence images and experimental data to help compare PTEN-knockout and control samples.

Climate Resilient Agricultural Engineering (CReAtE) Lab

Dr. Eunyu Kim · Duke Kunshan University, China

I work on identifying cultured microalgae. I assemble and interpret Sanger sequences, compare them with reference sequences, and build phylogenetic trees to evaluate possible species assignments. I also review research on microalgae and biophotovoltaics, including how photosynthetic organisms transfer electrons to electrodes.
